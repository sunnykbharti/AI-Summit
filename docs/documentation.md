## 📌 1. Executive Summary

Rural primary health centers (PHCs) across developing regions face a critical structural bottleneck: while frontline health workers are trained in basic patient care and first aid, qualified doctors are frequently unavailable on-site. Villagers experiencing acute or worsening conditions are forced to travel several hours to urban hospitals, leading to delayed treatments, escalation of minor conditions, and unnecessary economic distress.

**Nirmaya** (derived from the Sanskrit concept meaning *"free from disease"*) is an end-to-end, AI-powered Virtual Village Clinic platform designed by **Team Anomalies** for **BOBHACKS26**. Nirmaya bridges the rural healthcare access gap by providing frontline health workers with an intelligent clinical decision-support co-pilot, multi-modal document OCR, protocol-driven first-aid workflows, and asynchronous tele-triage connected to remote physicians—even in zero-bandwidth environments.

## 🎯 2. Problem Statement & Clinical Scope

### The Problem
* **Doctor Scarcity:** Trained health workers can collect basic vitals and provide initial aid, but cannot legally or clinically issue full consultations or prescriptions.
* **Connectivity Hurdles:** High-bandwidth video streaming often fails in remote villages during emergencies.
* **Literacy & Language Barriers:** Health workers and patients require native language interactions rather than complex medical jargon.
* **Safety & Hallucination Risks:** Generic AI tools risk hallucinating inappropriate drug dosages or misdiagnosing critical conditions.

### Nirmaya's Solution
Nirmaya transforms local health posts into reliable virtual clinics using a strictly guarded **Doctor-in-the-Loop** model. The AI collects, structures, and triages multi-modal patient data into concise clinical briefs (SOAP notes), allowing remote doctors to review and approve care plans in under 30 seconds.
